Equal card rectangles hide unequal internal tracks. Source/factory probes do not prove organic data or seen-state. Initial screenshots do not prove loading/scroll/keyboard behavior.

The singleton oracle derives ordinary width rather than comparing a card to itself. Breadcrumb occlusion is measured independently of a clear H1. Those fixes narrow specific false positives, not every product defect.
